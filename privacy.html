Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

The short version: the jobs, customers, prices, payments and other business information you enter in Coatwright are saved on your device and backed up privately to your Coatwright account, so they are safe if you lose your phone and available on every device you sign in on. Other users can't see them, and we never sell or share them or use them for advertising. Your account (your email, your name if you sign in with Google, and your trial and subscription status) is also stored online. If you subscribe, our payment provider handles your payment.

1. Information you enter in the app

When you use the Coatwright app, the information you enter (your business details and logo, customer names and contact details, quotes, jobs, payments, expenses and settings) is saved in your web browser's storage on your device, so the app works without an internet connection.

Cloud backup and sync. While you are signed in, the app also saves a copy of this information to your account, stored with our database provider Supabase (servers in the United States), and keeps it in sync across the devices you sign in on. Changes made offline upload the next time you're online. The copy is protected by your sign-in: database rules allow only your account to read or change it, and it is encrypted in transit and at rest. Our team does not look at it, except when you ask us for help with your account, to keep the service secure, or when the law requires it. Your sign-in details and subscription status are not part of this copy.

Usage counts. While you are signed in, the app also records which features you use, for example that a quote was started or sent, a job was marked won or paid, an invoice was made, or the blueprint takeoff was used, along with the time and simple labels such as the job type. It does not record your customers' names, addresses or contact details, your prices, or what is in your jobs. We use these counts to understand how Coatwright is used, fix problems and decide what to build next. They are stored with your account (Supabase), never sold or used for advertising, and deleted with your account.

If you delete a job or expense, it is removed from your account's copy as well. When your account is deleted, its copy is deleted with it. Signing out leaves the information on that device; clearing your browser data removes it from that device only.

2. Backups and exports

You can also create a backup file or spreadsheet exports from the app. These files are saved to your device and go only where you send them (for example, to your email or your accountant). Please keep backups somewhere safe, because they contain your customers' contact details.

3. Sending quotes and invoices

When you email, text or share a quote or invoice, Coatwright opens your own email, messaging or sharing app with the content filled in. The message is sent by that app, not by Coatwright, and is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

4. Your account

To use Coatwright you create an account with your email address (we email you a sign-in code) or with Google. We store, with our database and sign-in provider Supabase (servers in the United States): your email address, your name if Google provides it, when your free trial started, and when you signed up and last signed in. We use this to run your account, enforce one free trial per person, and contact you about your account. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us under Google's privacy policy; we don't receive your Google password.

To prevent repeat free trials, we keep a record of the email addresses that have started a trial, even if an account is later deleted. You can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing us.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold through Stripe, which processes your payment as the seller of record. Stripe collects the details needed to bill you (such as your name, email, billing address and payment card) under its own privacy policy. When you subscribe, Stripe tells us your plan, its status and renewal date, and the email used at checkout, and we store these with your account (in Supabase) so the app can unlock Coatwright Pro. If you are a founding member, we also store your founding-member number and send you a welcome email. We never see your full card number. Your jobs, customers and other business information are not sent to Stripe.

Your free-trial start date is stored on your device so the app can count the 14 days.

6. Your customers' information

If you enter information about your customers, you are responsible for having a good reason to keep it and for handling it in line with the laws that apply to your business.

7. Website hosting and fonts

The Coatwright website and app are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most websites, the hosting provider may record technical information such as IP addresses when pages are loaded, under GitHub's privacy statement. To count visits, this website (not the app) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or track you across other websites; it records things like the page viewed, the site that referred you, and your country, browser and device type, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. This marketing site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address under Google's privacy policy. The app itself has no advertising trackers and shares nothing with advertisers; the only usage information it keeps is the feature counts described in section 1.

8. Children

Coatwright is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account and its backup for as long as you have an account. You can ask us to delete your account and all of its information at any time by emailing us; we keep only the record of trial email addresses described above. If we add features that store new kinds of information, we will update this policy first.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

11. Contact
