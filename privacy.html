Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version: the jobs, customers, prices, payments and other business information you enter in Coatwright are stored on your own device. Coatwright does not have accounts, does not upload that information to our servers, and does not sell or share it.

1. Information you enter in the app

When you use the Coatwright app, the information you enter (your business details and logo, customer names and contact details, quotes, jobs, payments, expenses and settings) is saved in your web browser's storage on your device. It stays there until you delete it, clear your browser data, or uninstall the app. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you if it is lost.

2. Backups and exports

You can create a backup file or spreadsheet exports from the app. These files are saved to your device and go only where you send them (for example, to your email or your accountant). Please keep backups somewhere safe, because they contain your customers' contact details.

3. Sending quotes and invoices

When you email, text or share a quote or invoice, Coatwright opens your own email, messaging or sharing app with the content filled in. The message is sent by that app, not by Coatwright, and is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

4. Your customers' information

If you enter information about your customers, you are responsible for having a good reason to keep it and for handling it in line with the laws that apply to your business.

5. Website hosting and fonts

The Coatwright website and app are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most websites, the hosting provider may record technical information such as IP addresses when pages are loaded, under GitHub's privacy statement. This marketing site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address under Google's privacy policy. The app itself does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

6. Children

Coatwright is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Future features

Planned features such as accounts and cloud sync would store some information on our servers so it can be available on more than one device. Before any such feature is launched, we will update this policy to explain what is stored and why, and those features will be optional.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at coatwrightapp@gmail.com.
