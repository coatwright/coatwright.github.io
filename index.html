Features

Everything a painting business runs on

From the first walkthrough to the final payment, in one simple app on your phone.

Accurate quotes in minutes

Tap rooms or enter the home's square footage. Coatwright measures walls, ceilings and trim, rounds paint to whole gallons, and applies your labor rate, production speeds and markup.

  • Quick ballpark by square footage, or exact room-by-room
  • Interior, exterior, cabinets, garage floors, decks and fences
  • Ceiling height, coats, wall condition and stories all priced in
  • 15 commercial property types with their own areas

Quotes and invoices customers trust

Every line item, your logo, deposit and terms. Email, text or save a PDF before you leave the driveway, then turn the won job into an invoice in one tap.

  • Clean, itemized estimates with a signature line
  • Invoices with payments received and balance due
  • Quotes marked "Sent" automatically so nothing slips

Know your real numbers

Log payments and real costs as you go. Coatwright shows what you actually made, your true hourly rate, and whether your quotes are covering your time.

  • Yearly profit after paint, helpers and business expenses
  • Win rate, average job, money owed and quotes awaiting an answer
  • Actual vs. estimated hours, with plain-English tips
  • Spreadsheet exports for your accountant

FAQ

Questions painters ask

How much does Coatwright cost?

$24 a month, or $199 a year (just $16.58 a month). Our first 100 customers can lock in a founding-member price of $14 a month for life. Every plan includes every feature.

How does the free trial work?

You get every feature free for 14 days, with no credit card needed. If you don't subscribe, your jobs, customers and quotes stay on your phone: you can still view, back up and export them, you just can't create new quotes or invoices.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in a couple of taps from your subscription page. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

Do I need to download an app or create an account?

No. Open it in your phone's browser and start. To use it like an app, tap Share → "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone, or "Install app" on Android.

Will the prices match my market?

You set your own labor rate, markup, minimum job, tax, deposit, paint and material costs, and how fast you work. Every quote uses your numbers.

What kinds of jobs can I quote?

Interior rooms, whole-home ballparks, exteriors, decks, fences, doors, shutters, garage and basement floors (paint, epoxy or flake), cabinets, wallpaper and popcorn removal, drywall repair, and 15 types of commercial property.

Where is my data stored? What if I change phones?

On your device. Use Settings → Back up now to save a file, then Restore on your new phone. Cloud sync is coming with Pro.

How do I get help?

Email us anytime at coatwrightapp@gmail.com. We read every message, and feature requests from painters shape what we build next.

Can I send invoices and track payments?

Yes. Turn any won job into an invoice, log deposits and payments, and see what's still owed across all your jobs.
