Tap rooms or enter the home's square footage. Coatwright measures walls, ceilings and trim, rounds paint to whole gallons, and applies your labor rate, production speeds and markup.

FAQ

Questions painters ask

Is Coatwright really free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free and includes unlimited quotes, invoices, job tracking and stats. A paid Pro plan with cloud sync and online payments is planned; Starter will stay free.

Do I need to download an app or create an account?

No. Open it in your phone's browser and start. To use it like an app, tap Share → "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone, or "Install app" on Android.

Will the prices match my market?

You set your own labor rate, markup, minimum job, tax, deposit, paint and material costs, and how fast you work. Every quote uses your numbers.

What kinds of jobs can I quote?

Interior rooms, whole-home ballparks, exteriors, decks, fences, doors, shutters, garage and basement floors (paint, epoxy or flake), cabinets, wallpaper and popcorn removal, drywall repair, and 15 types of commercial property.

Where is my data stored? What if I change phones?

On your device. Use Settings → Back up now to save a file, then Restore on your new phone. Cloud sync is coming with Pro.

How do I get help?

Email us anytime at coatwrightapp@gmail.com. We read every message, and feature requests from painters shape what we build next.

Can I send invoices and track payments?

Yes. Turn any won job into an invoice, log deposits and payments, and see what's still owed across all your jobs.
