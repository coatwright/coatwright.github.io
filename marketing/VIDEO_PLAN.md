# Coatwright — Launch Video Plan

**Goal:** get painters to open **coatwright** (link in bio) and send their first quote.
**Who we're talking to:** new and solo painters (1–3 years in), small crews, and handymen who paint.
**What they care about:** winning jobs, not underpricing, looking professional, knowing if they're actually making money.

---

## 1. The game plan

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Platforms | TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels (same video on all four) |
| Length | 15–35 seconds. The first 2 seconds decide everything |
| Format | Vertical 1080×1920, captions burned in (most people watch muted) |
| Pace | **1–2 videos a day** for the first 30 days. Consistent beats "flooding": platforms bury accounts that post lots of near-identical clips |
| Link | coatwright link in bio on every platform; say "link in bio" out loud and on screen |
| Handle | @coatwright everywhere (grab them first) |
| Tracking | Each week: views, profile visits, link clicks per video. Make more of the top 3, drop the bottom 3 |

### The four video types (rotate through them)
1. **App demo:** real screen recording of the app doing something fast. *Best for sign-ups.* (Two are already made, see §4.)
2. **Painter pain:** a relatable problem (underpricing, napkin quotes, chasing payments), then the app solves it.
3. **Money truth:** numbers that make painters think ("You made $38/hr, not $55").
4. **Job-site / AI scenes:** cinematic painting shots with voiceover, for reach and brand.

### Hook formulas that work
- "Stop pricing paint jobs like this ❌"
- "I quoted a 2,000 sq ft house in 60 seconds"
- "If you're a new painter, you're probably underpricing. Here's how I know"
- "Painters: this is how much you *actually* made last year"
- "POV: the customer asks for a quote and you send it before you leave the driveway"

## 3. AI video prompts (for job-site scenes)

Use any AI video generator (for example Google Veo, OpenAI Sora, Runway or Kling) for 5–8 second clips, then edit them with the app recordings in a free editor like CapCut. Generate several takes and keep the most realistic.

- **Cut-in close-up:** "Vertical 9:16 macro shot of a professional painter's angled brush cutting a perfectly straight line of soft white paint along a ceiling edge, natural window light, shallow depth of field, satisfying slow motion, realistic."
- **Exterior refresh:** "Vertical 9:16 shot of a two-story suburban house, half the siding faded gray-blue, half freshly painted deep navy with crisp white trim, a painter on a ladder, golden hour, realistic, smooth camera push-in."
- **Epoxy flake floor:** "Vertical 9:16 overhead shot of decorative flakes being broadcast onto a wet gray epoxy garage floor, flakes fluttering down, bright work lights, realistic, satisfying."
- **Painter in truck with phone:** "Vertical 9:16 shot inside a work truck, a painter in paint-speckled work clothes smiling at a phone, quote sent, morning light, realistic, handheld feel." (Then cut to the real app screen.)
- **Rolling a wall:** "Vertical 9:16 shot of a roller laying a smooth coat of warm greige paint on a living room wall, drop cloths on the floor, realistic, steady."

**Voiceover:** record it yourself on your phone (authentic works best), or use an AI voice tool. Keep it conversational.

**Rules to stay trustworthy:**
- Turn on each platform's **"AI-generated" label** for AI clips (TikTok, Instagram/Facebook and YouTube all have one, and require it for realistic AI content).
- **No fake testimonials or fake customers.** Use real painters once you have them; until then, show the product.
- App screen recordings should be real (the two demos are real recordings with sample data).

**Weekly scorecard:** views · profile visits · link clicks · people who sent a first quote (ask in the app later). Keep what moves link clicks.
