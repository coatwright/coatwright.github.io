Set up Coatwright for commercial bids

About 2 minutes, on a computer. Coatwright works out of the box; these numbers make every bid priced your way.
Watch the video: coatwright-commercial-setup.mp4 (about 1 minute).

  1. Open Settings → Commercial

    Sign in at getcoatwright.com/app, click the ⚙️ Settings icon (top right), scroll down and click 🏬 Commercial.

  2. Quick setup

    • Labour rate: your commercial rate per hour. Fill it in even if it's the same as residential.
    • Overhead % and Profit %
    • Usual wall height
    • I price by: Labour hours or Unit prices ($ per sq ft, per door)

  3. If you know them (optional)

    How fast you paint walls (sq ft per hour, per coat), and what you pay for wall and ceiling paint. These move the price the most. Everything else already has typical commercial values.

  4. Fine-tune any time (optional)

    Fine-tune your paint systems: ceilings, doors, frames, dryfall, block, epoxy… each with its speed, paint use, paint price and unit price. Proposal wording: your inclusions, exclusions and clarifications.

  5. Start a bid

    ＋ New Quote → Commercial → I have the drawings. Every new blueprint takeoff starts with your numbers.

  6. Every job is different

    Change anything on a single bid (rates, any paint system): it only affects that bid. Found a better number? Click ⭐ Save as my default and it's used on every bid after that. Bids you already made never change.

Tip: Already measuring in Adobe or Bluebeam? In a takeoff, click ⌨️ Type a number under any paint system to enter your totals and get the same bid.
